Robust covariance estimation for portfolios

Compares the sample covariance with four regularised estimators, including Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage, random-matrix eigenvalue clipping and a PCA factor model, on 428 S&P 500 stocks. Tested in a walk-forward backtest with transaction costs and on a second dataset spanning the 2008 crisis; bootstrap intervals and a deflated Sharpe ratio show which differences hold up.

Python, NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib, pytest
